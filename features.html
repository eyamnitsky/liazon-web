Features

Everything Iris does to keep scheduling fast, human, and painless.

Effortless scheduling over email

Iris lives in email - where scheduling already happens. No links to click, no new tools to learn, no portals to log into. Just CC Iris and let her handle the coordination.

Value: zero behavior change for participants, dramatically faster scheduling.

Natural language understanding

Iris understands how people actually talk about time: "Next week works", "Anytime after lunch", "These should be fine". She interprets vague, partial, or messy replies and turns them into real availability.

Value: fewer follow-ups, fewer misunderstandings, less back-and-forth.

Multi-party coordination

Iris handles conversations with multiple participants across organizations. She gathers availability, proposes times, resolves conflicts, and confirms once everyone agrees.

Value: one thread instead of a scheduling nightmare.

Rescheduling, naturally

Plans change. Iris understands rescheduling requests and smoothly restarts coordination without losing context.

Value: meetings stay accurate without manual cleanup.

Calendar-aware scheduling

Iris avoids conflicts by checking calendars for licensed users, ensuring proposed times actually work.

Value: no double-booking, no awkward "sorry, I'm actually busy then" moments.

Works across organizations

Participants don’t need accounts, logins, or integrations. They just reply to email.

Value: frictionless scheduling with customers, partners, and candidates.

Secure by design

Iris only accesses calendars for licensed users in your organization and never exposes internal availability details externally.

Value: enterprise-grade trust without enterprise-grade complexity.
