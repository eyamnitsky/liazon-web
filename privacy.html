Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Liazon, LLC (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use Liazon (the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you provide when you:

  • Create an account
  • Connect an email account or calendar
  • Interact with the Service

This may include:

  • Name and email address
  • Account preferences and configuration settings
  • Calendar availability and event metadata

1.2 Email and Message Data

The Service processes email messages temporarily in order to perform user-requested actions such as scheduling, proposing times, or responding within an existing email thread.

We do not store email message contents or bodies.

The only email-related information stored in our systems is limited message metadata, such as:

  • Message identifiers
  • Sender and recipient addresses
  • Timestamps
  • Thread or conversation identifiers

All stored metadata is encrypted at rest and in transit and retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

If you connect third-party services (such as email or calendar providers), we may receive limited information required to operate the Service, including:

  • Email headers and metadata
  • Calendar availability and event details

We access the minimum data necessary to perform the requested functionality.

1.4 Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device and browser type
  • Log and usage data

This information is used for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Provide and operate the Service
  • Facilitate scheduling and meeting coordination
  • Generate user-initiated email responses
  • Maintain conversation context using encrypted metadata
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate abuse or misuse
  • Monitor system performance and reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use personal data for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

3. Email and Communications

Emails sent by the Service are:

  • User-initiated and transactional in nature
  • Sent only to participants involved in an existing scheduling conversation
  • Directly related to a communication initiated by the user

We do not send bulk, promotional, or unsolicited emails.

Recipients may request that assistant-generated emails stop at any time, and such requests will be honored promptly.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data.

We may share information only:

  • With trusted service providers that support our infrastructure (such as cloud hosting and email delivery)
  • To comply with applicable laws or lawful requests
  • To protect the security, integrity, and rights of Liazon, LLC and its users

All service providers are contractually required to protect data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data and encrypted message metadata only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Service
  • Maintain conversation continuity
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce our agreements

Users may request deletion of their data at any time, subject to legal or operational requirements.

6. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

All stored metadata is encrypted. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. GDPR and International Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the following applies:

7.1 Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:

  • Performance of a contract (providing the Service)
  • Legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, and service reliability)
  • User consent where required

7.2 Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data (data portability)

Requests can be submitted using the contact information below.

7.3 Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your jurisdiction, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards to protect such transfers.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
