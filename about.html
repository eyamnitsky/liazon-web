About Liazon

Liazon helps people schedule meetings without the back-and-forth. We built Liazon because calendars are rigid, inboxes are chaotic, and coordination still takes far more time than it should. Instead of links, forms, and endless replies, Liazon works directly through email—the place where scheduling already happens. You CC Liazon on a message. Liazon takes it from there.

Our Philosophy

We believe:

  • Software should adapt to people—not the other way around.
  • Automation should feel human, not robotic.
  • Trust is earned through restraint, not data hoarding.

Liazon is intentionally focused. It does one thing extremely well—and stays out of the way.
